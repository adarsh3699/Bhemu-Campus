Privacy Policy

UMS Data Sync — Chrome Extension

Last updated: June 25, 2026

What this extension does

UMS Data Sync fetches your academic data (grades, marks, and attendance) from the LPU University Management System (ums.lpu.in) and saves it to your personal Bhemu Calculator account.

Data we access

  • Grades and marks from your UMS results page
  • Attendance records from UMS APIs

How your data is stored

Your academic data is stored in your own Firebase account, linked to your Google sign-in. Only you can access your data. No data is stored on any third-party server controlled by us.

Data sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to any third party. Your data stays in your own account.

Permissions used

  • storage — Save sync status locally in the browser
  • tabs — Open the UMS login page when needed
  • webNavigation — Detect when you've successfully logged into UMS
  • Host access to ums.lpu.in — Fetch your academic data from UMS
  • Host access to googleapis.com — Save data to your Firebase account

Data retention and deletion

Your data is retained in your Firebase account until you choose to delete it. You can delete your synced data at any time by:

  • Deleting your profile from within the Bhemu Calculator app
  • Deleting your account entirely, which removes all associated data

Uninstalling the extension does not delete previously synced data. You must delete it from the app.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access all data stored in your account (visible in the app)
  • Delete your data at any time
  • Stop syncing by uninstalling the extension

Children's privacy

This extension is intended for university students. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used this extension, please contact us and we will delete the data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
